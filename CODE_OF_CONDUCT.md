# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or level of participation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that supports this community include:

- communicating with empathy, patience, and respect;
- giving actionable feedback about the work rather than the person;
- acknowledging mistakes and accepting constructive correction;
- respecting privacy, consent, and the boundaries of other contributors;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing private communications, or sustained disruption.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project, including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project-related private communication.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct incidents privately through the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/achieve0410/slack-dashboard/security/advisories/new) and prefix the title with `Code of Conduct`. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. If the private form is unavailable, open an issue that asks the maintainer for a private contact channel without describing the incident.

Maintainers will review reports in confidence, may request additional context, and will take action proportionate to the behavior. Possible actions include a private warning, removal of content, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.
